# ICC Incoterms® 2020 Rules & Exporter Risk Allocations

## 1. Rules for Sea and Inland Waterway Transport
- **FOB (Free on Board)**: Exporter delivers goods on board the vessel nominated by the buyer at the named port of shipment (e.g. FOB Port Qasim). Risk transfers once goods are on board. Exporter handles origin export customs (WeBOC).
- **CFR (Cost and Freight)**: Exporter contracts and pays ocean freight to the destination port. Risk transfers when goods are loaded on board at the origin port.
- **CIF (Cost, Insurance & Freight)**: Exporter contracts ocean freight and procures Marine Cargo Insurance complying with Institute Cargo Clauses (C) or higher. Minimum insurance coverage must equal invoice amount + 10% (110% CIF value) in the contract currency.

## 2. Rules for Any Mode of Transport
- **EXW (Ex Works)**: Buyer bears all costs and risks from factory collection. Exporter has no export clearance obligation.
- **FCA (Free Carrier)**: Exporter clears goods for export and delivers to buyer's carrier at factory or dry port terminal.
- **CPT (Carriage Paid To)**: Exporter pays main carriage freight to destination.
- **CIP (Carriage and Insurance Paid To)**: Exporter pays main carriage and procures Institute Cargo Clauses (A) all-risk insurance coverage for 110% value.
- **DAP (Delivered at Place)**: Exporter bears all risks and costs to bring goods to destination place, unloaded. Buyer pays destination import duties.
- **DDP (Delivered Duty Paid)**: Exporter bears maximum obligation, paying destination import clearance, duties, and taxes.
