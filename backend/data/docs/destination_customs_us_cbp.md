# United States (US) CBP Import Customs & Security Regulations

## 1. Importer Security Filing (ISF 10+2 Rule — 19 CFR 149.2)
- For ocean containerized shipments to the United States, 10 mandatory data elements must be submitted to US Customs and Border Protection (CBP) at least **24 hours prior to loading** the vessel at the port of origin (e.g. Port Qasim / Karachi Port).
- Required data elements: Manufacturer / Supplier, Seller, Buyer, Ship-to party, Scheduled container stuffing location, Consolidator, Importer of Record Number, Consignee Number, Country of Origin, and 6-digit Harmonized Tariff Schedule (HTS) Code.

## 2. FDA Prior Notice (21 CFR Part 1)
- For agricultural, food, or dietary items (e.g. Basmati rice, pink salt), FDA Prior Notice must be submitted and confirmed prior to arrival.

## 3. Lacey Act Plant & Wood Declaration
- Applies to manufactured goods containing wood components (cutlery handles, musical instruments, sports wooden gear).
