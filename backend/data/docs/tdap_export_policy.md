# Trade Development Authority of Pakistan (TDAP) & Export Policy Order

## 1. Export Quality & Conformity Certification
- **Pakistan Standards & Quality Control Authority (PSQCA)**: Mandatory quality certification for processed goods, surgical equipment, sports goods, and technical textiles.
- **Department of Plant Protection (DPP)**: Mandatory Phytosanitary and Fumigation Certificates for all agricultural commodities (Super Kernel Basmati Rice, dried fruits, cotton products).
- **Marine Fisheries Department (MFD)**: Health certification and HACCP compliance for seafood exports.

## 2. Certificate of Origin Attestation
- **Chamber of Commerce Attestation**: Non-preferential Certificates of Origin must be attested by the local Chamber of Commerce & Industry (e.g. SCCI Sialkot, KCCI Karachi, FCCI Faisalabad).
- **Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MoFA) & Embassy Legalization**: Mandatory for exports to Middle East / GCC nations (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Qatar).
