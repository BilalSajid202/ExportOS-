# State Bank of Pakistan (SBP) Foreign Exchange Circulars & Operational Rules

## 1. SBP FE Circular No. 04 of 2020 — Realization Monitoring & EFE Settlement
- Mandates that Authorized Dealers actively monitor realization aging from Day 1 of Bill of Lading departure.
- At 90 days post-shipment, Authorized Dealers must issue automated pre-overdue advisory notices to the exporter.
- At 120 days post-shipment, Authorized Dealers are required to report unresolved export receivables to the SBP FEOD Overdue Portal.

## 2. Advance Payment Procedures & Electronic Form-R
- Where foreign buyers remit payments prior to shipment, the Authorized Dealer issues an Electronic Form-R (Proceeds Realization Certificate / PRC).
- The exporter must ship the underlying goods and generate the corresponding Electronic Form-E within one year of advance payment receipt.
- Upon export shipment, the Authorized Dealer reconciles the Form-E against the previously issued Form-R in the PSW portal.

## 3. Freight & Export Remittance Regulations
- Under CFR / CIF / CIP contracts, Authorized Dealers are authorized to remit outward ocean or air freight charges to foreign shipping lines or airline agents in USD/EUR against verified freight invoices and Master Bills of Lading.
- Foreign agent commission remittances are permitted up to 10% of FOB invoice value subject to contractual agency agreements.
