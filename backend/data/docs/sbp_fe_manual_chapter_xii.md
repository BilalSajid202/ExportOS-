# State Bank of Pakistan (SBP) Foreign Exchange Manual — Chapter XII (Exports)

## 1. Statutory Mandate & Authority
Under the Foreign Exchange Regulation Act (FERA), 1947, all exports from Pakistan are subject to mandatory monitoring by the State Bank of Pakistan (SBP) and the Foreign Exchange Operations Department (FEOD).

## 2. Electronic Form-E (EFE) via Pakistan Single Window (PSW)
- **Requirement**: Exporters must declare every export consignment electronically through an Electronic Form-E (EFE) via the Pakistan Single Window (PSW) system prior to filing the Goods Declaration (GD) with Pakistan Customs (WeBOC).
- **Authorized Dealer Validation**: The Authorized Dealer (commercial bank) must perform online verification and financial certification of the EFE before customs out-of-charge is granted.
- **Value Matching**: The FOB/CFR value declared on the Commercial Invoice and Customs GD must match or exceed the value approved on the Electronic Form-E.

## 3. Mandatory 120-Day Proceeds Realization Requirement (Para 6)
- **Realization Deadline**: Full export proceeds must be realized and repatriated into Pakistan within **one hundred and twenty (120) calendar days** from the date of transport shipment (date of Bill of Lading or Air Waybill departure).
- **Grace Period & Extension**: Extensions beyond 120 days require formal prior approval from the SBP Foreign Exchange Operations Department through the Authorized Dealer with supporting justification (e.g., buyer force majeure, international arbitration).
- **Statutory Offense**: Failure to realize export proceeds within 120 days without SBP extension places the exporter on the SBP Non-Compliant / Overdue Exporters List, triggering restrictions on new EFE issuance and statutory enforcement under FERA 1947.

## 4. Payment Modes Permitted under SBP Chapter XII
1. **Letter of Credit (LC)**: Irrevocable Documentary Credit issued by a first-class international bank.
2. **Advance Payment**: 100% pre-shipment wire transfer with mandatory e-Form R reconciliation.
3. **Documents Against Payment (DP / CAD)**: Sight collection handled via banking channels.
4. **Documents Against Acceptance (DA / Usance)**: Term collection with maturity not exceeding 120 days from shipment date.
5. **Open Account**: Permitted for verified multinational buyers with Authorized Dealer record-keeping.
