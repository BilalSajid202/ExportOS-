# European Union (EU) Destination Customs, GSP+ REX & REACH Regulations

## 1. Registered Exporter (REX) System for GSP+ Preference
- **System**: Exporters in Pakistan eligible for EU Generalised Scheme of Preferences (GSP+ zero-duty market access) must be registered under the EU REX system.
- **Statement on Origin**: Instead of a government-issued Certificate of Origin Form-A, the exporter issues a self-certified **Statement on Origin** directly on the commercial invoice or packing list.
- **Mandatory Statement Text**:
  > *"The exporter of the products covered by this document (customs authorization No PKREX...) declares that, except where otherwise clearly indicated, these products are of Pakistani preferential origin in accordance with rules of origin of the Generalised System of Preferences of the European Union."*

## 2. EU REACH (EC 1907/2006) Safety & Chemical Compliance
- Applies to textiles, leather footwear, sporting goods, and apparel.
- Exporter must provide chemical safety test reports confirming absence of restricted azo dyes, phthalates, lead, cadmium, and hexavalent chromium (Cr VI).

## 3. Packaging & Wood Fumigation (ISPM-15)
- All solid wood packaging materials (pallets, crates, dunnage) must be heat treated or fumigated and stamped with the official IPPC ISPM-15 seal.
